# Close-table story

- A table can remain playable while a close request is pending.
- The close request has a 120-minute deadline.
- Settlement is performed later and makes the final balances durable.
- A player can disconnect before the deadline and return after it but before
  settlement begins.
- The supplied business rules do not say whether that player may resume play,
  nor whether the deadline itself or the start of settlement ends that right.
